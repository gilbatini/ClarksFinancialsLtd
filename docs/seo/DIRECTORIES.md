## Exclusions and stop rules

- Skip directories with no public indexable listing, no correction path, copied/scraped data only, malware warnings, hidden recurring fees, paid-link requirements, or demands for borrower/customer information.
- Do not buy bulk citation packages, review packages, “guaranteed ranking,” or keyword-stuffed business names.
- Do not upload licence scans, IDs, tax records, bank details, or staff/customer personal data to a marketing directory.
- Paid tiers need a named owner, price, cancellation terms, expected measurable outcome, and approval before purchase.
- A regulatory directory mismatch is a compliance escalation, not an SEO editing task.
