# Customer review generation and response process

This process is for genuine, voluntary feedback. It must never affect an application, decision, loan term, servicing outcome, complaint, or access to support.

## Non-negotiable rules

- Ask only people with a genuine direct experience of Clarks.
- Ask for an **honest review**, never a positive or five-star review.
- Do not offer money, discounts, fee changes, gifts, faster service, favourable treatment, entry into a draw, or any other benefit.
- Do not ask someone to change or remove a negative review in exchange for any benefit.
- Do not screen by satisfaction before showing the public-review option (“review gating”). The same neutral path must be available regardless of sentiment.
- Staff, owners, close relatives, contractors, agencies, and anyone with an undisclosed conflict must not review Clarks.
- Never write, edit, translate, pre-fill, or post a customer's review. Do not provide suggested keywords or talking points.
- Never use bulk-created accounts, devices, locations, or a shared in-office device to post reviews.
- A customer may decline without follow-up pressure.

Google permits requests based on genuine experience but prohibits incentives, biased reviews, and rating manipulation: <https://support.google.com/contributionpolicy/answer/7400114> and <https://support.google.com/contributionpolicy/answer/16597558>.

## Eligibility and timing

A staff member may send one review request only when:

1. the person has completed a genuine service interaction;
2. the request is not tied to approval, disbursement, collections, restructuring, complaint resolution, or any disputed matter;
3. the approved channel can reach that person without exposing their borrower status to others;
4. marketing/contact permission and applicable privacy requirements have been checked; and
5. the person has not already received a request for the same interaction.

Do not request a review during financial distress, arrears/collections contact, a vulnerability disclosure, an active complaint, fraud handling, bereavement, or a regulatory dispute.

## Approved request flow

1. The GBP owner generates the official review link or QR code from the verified Business Profile. Google supports sharing it by email, WhatsApp, Facebook, receipt, or an in-store QR code: <https://support.google.com/business/answer/16816815>.
2. Compliance approves the message and channel.
3. Staff send one neutral request. Do not send the request in a group chat or expose any financial relationship.
4. Record only the send date, approved template/version, staff owner, and channel. Do not record or infer the rating the person will leave.
5. Do not chase an individual who declines or does not respond. A single general reminder may be approved as part of a service follow-up programme, never as pressure on one person.

Approved neutral wording:

> Thank you for sharing your time with Clarks Financials. If you would like to describe your genuine experience, you can leave an honest Google review here: [official review link]. Reviews are optional and do not affect any service or decision. Please do not include account, application, identification, payment, or other personal financial information.

The final link remains a placeholder until GBP ownership and verification are complete.

## Staff script for in-person use

“If you choose, you can use our official QR code to leave an honest review of your experience. It is optional and will not affect any service. Please do not include personal or financial details.”

If the person starts describing a complaint, stop the review request and direct the issue into the private complaint/support process.

## Monitoring and response workflow

- A named customer-service owner checks new reviews on business days after GBP access exists.
- Capture review URL, date, status, response owner, response date, and escalation category. Do not copy sensitive allegations into an unsecured tracker.
- Reply only when there is useful, approved information to add; a public reply is not required for every review.
- Replies must be short, professional, factual, non-promotional, and written as Clarks.
- Never confirm that the reviewer applied, borrowed, was approved/declined, owes money, made a payment, complained, or has any account relationship.
- Never disclose names beyond the public profile, phone numbers, email addresses, IDs, employer, amount, balance, term, dates, internal notes, or investigation details.

Google notes that replies are public, recommends protecting private information, and suggests moving complex issues to phone/email: <https://support.google.com/business/answer/3474050>.

## Response patterns

Positive or neutral feedback:

> Thank you for taking the time to share feedback. We appreciate your comments.

Service concern without sensitive detail:

> Thank you for raising this. We would like to understand the situation through a private channel. Please contact our team using the phone number or email shown on our official profile. For your privacy, please do not post application, account, payment, or identification details here.

Potentially false, abusive, or policy-violating content:

- do not accuse or argue publicly;
- preserve the review URL and a dated screenshot;
- check Google's current content policy;
- flag only where a documented policy ground applies;
- escalate threats, extortion, impersonation, fraud, doxxing, discrimination, regulatory allegations, or legal claims to compliance/security before responding.

Do not flag a review merely because it is negative. Google's general Maps contribution policy requires genuine, relevant content and provides reporting routes for violations: <https://support.google.com/contributionpolicy/answer/7422880>.

## Quality controls

- Quarterly, compliance samples requests and responses for consent, neutrality, privacy, incentive, and gating breaches.
- Revoke templates or QR materials immediately if the link, location, profile ownership, policy, or business identity changes.
- Train new staff before they can send requests or reply publicly.
- Report request count, voluntary response count where available, response coverage, escalation count, and policy removals. Do not set a target rating or pressure teams to generate positive sentiment.
- Keep records only for the approved retention period and restrict them to the minimum authorised staff.

## Accountabilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| GBP owner | Official review link/QR, account access, and platform actions |
| Customer service | Eligible neutral requests, monitoring, and approved replies |
| Compliance/privacy | Templates, training, sensitive escalations, audits, and retention |
| Security/legal | Fraud, impersonation, extortion, threats, and legal process |
| SEO/measurement | Aggregate reporting without reviewer profiling or rating manipulation |
