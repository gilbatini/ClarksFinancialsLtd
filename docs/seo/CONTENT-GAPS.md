## Editorial guardrails

- Never invent or extrapolate rates, fees, eligibility, licences, awards, approval timing, or customer counts.
- Every numeric or regulatory product claim needs a named source, owner, and review date.
- Avoid superlatives and unsourced comparisons.
- Separate general financial education from personalised financial advice.
- Route new educational pages through compliance before publication.
